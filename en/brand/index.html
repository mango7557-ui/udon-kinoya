Brand Story

We speak through our noodles.

Why Udon Kinoya focuses on the noodle itself instead of menu variety — the story of how the brand began.

Since the first bowl
物語のはじまり
Origin

A story that started
with one bowl.

Many restaurant brands try to keep customers with a wide variety of menu items. Udon Kinoya chose the opposite path. Instead of expanding the menu, we first set out to perfect a method for reproducing one bowl of udon at the same quality, every day.

The answer wasn't flashy toppings — it was the noodle itself. If the noodle is good, the broth and toppings only need to support it. That is the principle Udon Kinoya discovered.

キノヤの原則
Brand Principle

Focused on the essence of udon,
Kinoya's promise to customers.

Customer Promise

Devoted only to udon, striving forward,
to become the nation's best udon brand.

Udon Kinoya spends its time on noodle research rather than menu expansion. 13 months of noodle R&D and a 48-hour aging process exist to keep this promise.

10年、20年、それ以上
Legacy

An udon brand loved
for 10, 20 years and beyond.

In Japan, there are udon craftsmen who have kept their trade alive for 50, even 100 years. Udon Kinoya aims to be like them — making the same unwavering bowl and becoming a beloved udon shop that is your warm meal for years to come.
