Why franchise cost varies

Franchise cost depends on store size, whether new construction is required, the condition of an existing site (for conversion eligibility), and regional construction rates. Even at the same size, the final investment can vary significantly with interior wear and whether existing equipment can be reused.

Standard store (~30 pyeong)

Based on the head-office standard reference table for a 30-pyeong new store, the total reference investment is ₩124.85M (VAT included), reflecting limited support up to the 100th franchise location (30% off franchise fee, 40% off training fee). Actual cost varies with new-construction needs and site condition after on-site assessment.

Business Conversion

Choosing a conversion approach that reuses an existing restaurant's facilities can reduce investment compared to new construction (reference conversion cases without key money range around ₩72M–₩82M, such as Gayang Station, Guro Digital Complex, and Hanam Misa). The extent of savings depends on the condition and usable scope of the existing facility.
