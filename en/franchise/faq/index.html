Udon Kinoya Franchise FAQ

Answered by category: brand & product, franchise cost, revenue, operations, store, and head-office support.

Udon Kinoya is an udon specialty brand and franchise built on 48-hour aged noodles and a standardized head-office operating system.
See the brand story
After 13 months of noodle R&D, we finalized a blend that is cold-aged for 48 hours and standardized into a one-pack noodle roughly 80cm long, designed to reproduce handmade quality at the store level.
See The Noodle in detail
Udon Kinoya determined that a 48-hour cold aging process after kneading is necessary to consistently reproduce a chewy texture, and fixed this as our standard process.
