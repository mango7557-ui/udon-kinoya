What is Udon Kinoya

Udon Kinoya is an udon specialty brand and franchise built on
48-hour aged noodles and a standardized head-office operating system.

The head office standardizes and supplies noodle-making, aging, and packing, while stores focus on the boiling-and-plating side of cooking. This structure aims for an operating difficulty accessible even to first-time owners.
