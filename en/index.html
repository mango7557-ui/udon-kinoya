Brand Story

Our Story

A story that started
with one bowl.

Udon Kinoya began with the simple goal of making one great bowl of udon. Many recipes for delicious udon already exist, but reproducing it every single day at the same quality was an entirely different problem.

Instead of expanding the menu, Udon Kinoya chose to solve this problem through an obsessive focus on the noodle itself.

Read the full brand story
麺への集中
The Noodle

Great udon
always starts with the noodle.

Udon Kinoya operates its own noodle-making factory. The head office kneads, ages, and packs 48-hour aged noodles every day and supplies them to every store — stores only need to boil and plate with a verified process.

We reproduce handmade-quality noodles in a one-pack format of roughly 80cm, letting stores run a fast, simple, high-turnover kitchen.
