The Noodle

Great udon always starts with the noodle.

Udon Kinoya operates its own noodle factory and supplies standardized 48-hour aged noodles in a one-pack format to every store.

Q&A

Do stores need to make their own noodles?

No. Udon Kinoya supplies standardized 48-hour aged noodles from the head office, so stores never need to knead or make noodles themselves every day. The head office delivers noodles that are already kneaded, aged, and packed — stores only need to boil and plate through a verified process.

熟成の理由
Why 48 Hours

48 hours that create
a chewy delight.

Water, salt, flour. The simpler the ingredients, the stricter the principles. Udon Kinoya decided that 48 hours of cold aging after kneading is required to complete today's chewy texture, and fixed this process as our standard.

It took 13 months of noodle R&D to finalize the current blend and aging method.
