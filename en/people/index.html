People

A system is, ultimately, built by people.

Udon Kinoya's standardized operating system is built together by head-office teams covering the areas below.

R&D

Noodle-making & R&D

Researches and standardizes the noodle blend and aging process. After 13 months of R&D, we finalized the current 48-hour aging process.

Development

Trade Area Development

Analyzes prospective trade areas and reviews sites in line with our territory protection policy.

Supervising

Supervising & Operations

Regularly checks stores after opening and supports quality management and cost management.

Marketing

New Menu & Marketing

Develops new menu items in line with trends and runs marketing to increase sales.
